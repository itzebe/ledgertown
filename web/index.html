How a merchant decides (and who has the last word)

Each shop runs one fixed, readable policy over its own on-chain state:

  1. Restock toward twice its base daily demand, but only with cash above a reserve of 2× fixed cost.
  2. If a rival quotes the same good cheaper, undercut by one 200 bp step (markup floor 500 bp); otherwise relax markup back toward 2200 bp.

The merchant only proposes (merchant_decide, signed by its own NEAR account). The contract re-checks every proposal against real cash and stock and records ACCEPTED or REJECTED — that is the decision shown on each shop.

A shop below its reserve stops buying; below fixed cost for three days with no sales it closes; funding it back to 2× fixed cost reopens it. Both are on-chain events.
