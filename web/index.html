How the town works

  1. The town is a persistent market whose rules live in a NEAR testnet smart contract. The contract — not the browser — is the economic authority.
  2. Four merchants each run one shop and specialise in one good: miller → grain, smith → iron, weaver → cloth, brewer → ale.
  3. You gather goods for free, sell them to a merchant for credits, buy stock to trade, or advance the market day. Advancing a day runs one full settlement cycle for every shop.
  4. A merchant can close if it cannot cover its daily fixed cost for several days — you will see a closed badge and a CLOSED event in the ledger.
  5. Investment revives it. Fund a closed shop to at least twice its fixed cost and the contract reopens it, recording a REOPENED event.
  6. Testnet only. Credits are game credits on NEAR testnet, not money. Nothing here touches mainnet or real funds.
